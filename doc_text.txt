Chapter 1 – Introduction
1.1 Introduction
The Bus Smart System App is an advanced smart transportation management application designed to improve public and private bus transportation systems using IoT technologies, GPS tracking, cloud computing, and mobile applications. The application allows passengers, drivers, and transport management authorities to monitor buses in real time and manage transportation operations efficiently.
The system provides live bus tracking, smart attendance management, digital ticketing, route optimization, fuel monitoring, emergency alerts, driver behavior analysis, and passenger notifications through a user-friendly mobile application. The Bus Smart System App improves communication between passengers, bus drivers, and transportation management systems.
________________


1.2 Background of the Study
Modern transportation systems are moving toward digital transformation through smart technologies and intelligent transportation systems (ITS). Traditional bus systems often face problems such as delays, lack of route transparency, poor passenger communication, inefficient fuel management, and limited safety monitoring.
With the rapid growth of IoT devices, GPS modules, mobile applications, and cloud-based systems, smart transportation solutions are becoming essential for improving public transport efficiency and passenger satisfaction.
The Bus Smart System App is designed to address these transportation challenges by integrating smart monitoring systems with mobile and cloud technologies.
________________


1.3 Problem Statement
Traditional bus transportation systems face multiple challenges such as:
* No real-time bus tracking
* Passenger uncertainty regarding bus arrival times
* Poor communication between drivers and passengers
* Manual ticketing systems
* Lack of safety monitoring
* Fuel misuse and inefficient route management
* Limited emergency response systems
* No centralized monitoring platform
* Difficulty in tracking school or company buses
A smart bus management system is required to improve transportation efficiency, safety, and convenience.
